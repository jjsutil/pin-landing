## Public byline

## Grounded facts

Bullet list of career/role facts an agent may cite when writing in this voice — dated,
and traceable to the person or the owner directly (not inferred, not embellished). This
is the *only* source of specifics; nothing outside this list gets attributed to them.

## Voice

Tone, sentence rhythm, what this person would and wouldn't say. 2-3 example sentences
if possible. The goal is a post a reader who knows this person would believe they wrote.

## Pillars owned

Which pillar(s) of `planning/plans/E01-editorial-calendar.md` this voice writes for, and
why the fit is real rather than assigned for variety.

## Boundaries

third parties, anecdotes beyond the grounded facts above. Note any fact that is
time-sensitive and needs re-confirming before reuse in a later post.
